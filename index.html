About Dept. of CARE

A dedicated centre aimed at strengthening the technological capacity of Textile, Apparel, and Fashion MSMEs in Tirupur. CARE provides incubation support, machine development, and innovative product testing.

Key Focus Areas

Research & Development of new products, process automation, and machinery development for apparel production.

Click to read details

Our focus revolves around innovating and introducing industrial-grade machinery and technical textiles to local MSMEs in Tirupur.

  • Machine Development: Design of specialized textile machinery like Double Jacquard electronic looms.
  • Process Automation: Enhancing manufacturing speeds while maintaining energy efficiency.
  • R&D in Technical Textiles: Development of flame-resistant (PBI-blended) outer shells and protective fabrics.
  • Prototyping & Testing: Complete lab testing for industrial feasibility.

Technology Transfer

Transferring advanced prototype technology to industrial applications, ensuring cost efficiency for local manufacturers.

Click to read details

We facilitate seamless technical adoption by enabling local MSMEs to integrate state-of-the-art tech efficiently.

  • Commercialization: Converting research prototypes into market-ready industrial setups.
  • Cost Reduction: Optimizing production cycles to lower cost-per-unit for manufacturers.
  • Prototype Audits: Conducting pilot scale validation before high-volume commercial runs.

Policy Support

Support for R&D policy implementation, preliminary assessment for Govt policies, and skill development programs.

Click to read details

Dept. of CARE acts as a bridge between MSMEs, research institutions, and state/central government bodies.

  • Government Schemes: Guidance for EDII-TN (IVP), DST, DC MSME Hackathon proposals.
  • Handloom & Cluster Training: Hands-on skill enhancement workshops for artisans and operators.
  • DPR Preparation: Detailed Project Reports to help MSMEs apply for government grants.

Collaborative Activities with Govt & Institutions

DST Scheme

Mat Loom Upgradation for Rural Women

Sanctioned Budget: ₹56 Lakhs (3-Year Plan)

Upgraded electronic loom prototype successfully installed and tested at NIFT-TEA center to boost rural employment and handloom efficiency. This initiative transforms conventional weaving workflows into automated processes. It provides structured skill orientation sessions for rural women artisans to seamlessly operate modern machinery. Furthermore, it creates sustainable livelihood opportunities across target clusters in Tamil Nadu. The project stands as a benchmark for technology integration in decentralized sectors.

MSME Hackathon 5.0

PBI-Blended Firefighter Suit

Sanctioned Amount: ₹12.75 Lakhs

High-performance flame-resistant outer shell fabric developed for firefighter suits using advanced PBI-blended yarn formulations. The material undergoes rigorous thermal endurance testing to ensure maximum safety under extreme rescue environments. Specialized laboratory testing evaluates heat attenuation, tensile strength, and chemical splash resistance properties. Local manufacturing units receive technical know-how to scale up commercial production affordably. This innovation directly addresses critical safety gear gaps faced by national emergency response squads.

MSME Hackathon 4.0

Autoclave for Fabric Finishing

Industrial prototype design and thermal treatment autoclave development for high-density fabric finishing.

Engineered specifically to optimize high-pressure treatments required for technical textile finishing operations. The system incorporates precision temperature and pressure regulatory controllers to maintain uniform quality parameters. Energy conservation mechanisms are integrated to lower utility consumption during continuous factory shifts. Comprehensive pilot trials demonstrate significant improvements in fabric dimensional stability and finish consistency. The successful prototype offers local processing houses a cost-effective domestic automation alternative.

NABARD Project

Wastewater & DPR Training

Hands-on skill training conducted for 80 Wastewater Technicians across 2 batches, along with DPR preparation support.

Comprehensive training modules developed to address crucial environmental compliance needs in textile clusters. Participants learn advanced effluent treatment monitoring, chemical dosing management, and water recycling techniques. Expert-led sessions guide entrepreneurs through detailed project report (DPR) preparation for funding approvals. Field visits to operational treatment plants provide candidates with practical troubleshooting experience. The program successfully empowers local technicians to maintain sustainable ecological practices in units.

TNSRLM Scheme

Hardware Cluster & CRP Training

2-Day Master Trainer Training program conducted for CRP Coat manufacturing and Handloom cluster accreditation.

Focused capacity building initiative designed to upgrade the technical competence of community resource persons. Practical workshops cover specialized coat construction methodologies, quality checking, and standardized stitching processes. Guidance is provided on meeting institutional cluster accreditation requirements and compliance norms. Trainees receive extensive instruction on transferring acquired sewing skills to grassroots manufacturing groups. This creates an organized network of skilled master trainers supporting regional apparel initiatives.

KVIC Initiatives

Credit Awareness & Product Exhibition

Awareness workshops and product displays organized in coordination with KVIC for rural artisans and textiles.

Special outreach events organized to educate traditional artisans about financial assistance and subsidy schemes. Financial literacy sessions cover banking procedures, mudra loans, and digital credit management facilities. Curated product exhibitions offer rural creators a direct platform to showcase indigenous textile designs to buyers. Interactive networking sessions connect artisans with leading apparel brands for potential market linkages. The initiative effectively bridges information gaps and promotes inclusive economic growth.
